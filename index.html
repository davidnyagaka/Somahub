    📝 Study Notes

    Simple ways to make revision more effective.

    📖 Active Recall

    Close your book and try to explain a concept from memory. Then check your notes and correct any mistakes.

    🗓️ Spaced Revision

    Review a topic over several days instead of leaving everything until the last minute.

    🤝 Study Groups

    Discuss difficult concepts, ask each other questions and explain ideas in your own words.
