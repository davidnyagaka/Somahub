    About SomaHub

    SomaHub is an educational platform concept designed to bring students and teachers together through learning resources, revision notes, quizzes and goal setting.

    Our mission is to make learning more organised, accessible and collaborative.

    This is an early-stage MVP. Notes and goals are saved locally in the browser on the device being used. A shared student-teacher account system is not yet included.
