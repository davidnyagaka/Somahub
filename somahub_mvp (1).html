🌍 Geography: The Water Cycle

The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between Earth's surface and the atmosphere.

  • Evaporation: liquid water changes into water vapour.
  • Condensation: water vapour cools and forms droplets.
  • Precipitation: water falls from clouds as rain, snow, or hail.
  • Collection: water gathers in rivers, lakes, and oceans.

🇫🇷 French: Regular -ER Verbs

To conjugate a regular verb ending in -er, remove -er and add the appropriate ending.

Example: parler (to speak)

  • Je parle — I speak.
  • Tu parles — You speak.
  • Il/Elle parle — He/She speaks.
  • Nous parlons — We speak.
  • Vous parlez — You speak.
  • Ils/Elles parlent — They speak.

📚 Study Skills: Active Recall

Active recall means trying to remember information without looking at your notes.

  1. Read a short topic carefully.
  2. Close your book or notes.
  3. Explain or write what you remember.
  4. Check your notes and correct mistakes.
